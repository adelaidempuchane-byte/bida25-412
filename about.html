About Us

At Lee Florists, we believe flowers are more than just decorations — they are expressions of love, joy, and comfort. Our mission is to bring beauty and nature closer to you, whether it’s for a wedding, a corporate event, or a simple gesture of kindness.

Founded in Botswana, we are proud to serve our community with locally inspired arrangements and reliable delivery. We strive to make every bouquet a story, connecting people through the timeless language of flowers.

With secure payment options like Orange Money, MyZaka, EFT, Visa, and Cash on Delivery, shopping with us is simple and trustworthy.
